UIU Waivers, Thesis & Retake Policy

UIU Minimum 9-Credit Requirement: Undergraduate students must register for at least 9 credits (14 credits for Pharmacy) to be eligible for scholarships.
UIU Thesis & Waiver Policy: Scholarships and waivers never apply to Thesis, Project, Internship, Retake, or Repeat courses. For a 6-credit thesis (taken as 2 credits each trimester), full 100% tuition is charged for the 2 thesis credits with 0% waiver. Retaken courses receive a 50% waiver.
